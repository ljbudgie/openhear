## Day-to-day impact, in my own words

In a quiet room, facing one person, I follow most of what is said by lip-reading, though it tires me. In groups or with background noise I lose the thread quickly. I often realise afterwards that I misheard a detail, so a written note of what was agreed matters to me.

**What helps**

- A written summary after a meeting
- A quiet, well-lit room
- Time to read and reply before decisions

**What does not help**

- Speakers who turn away or cover their mouths
- Being expected to answer straight away

_Self-reported. Recorded 2026-08; last reviewed 2026-11._
