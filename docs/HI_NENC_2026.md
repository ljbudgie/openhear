# Health Innovation NENC market research — September 2026

External note. Not the first page. A first-time user does not need this.

Health Innovation North East and North Cumbria prepared *Market Research: OpenHear* (September 2026) for The Burgess Principle Limited. The PDF is not in this repository. It stays with the people it was written for unless they say it can be published.

What the review concluded, in short:

- OpenHear is not a hearing aid. It classifies environmental sound and communicates it by wrist vibration.
- The unmet need (awareness of doorbells, alarms, traffic, and other everyday sounds) is supported in the literature. Existing products mostly alert on one event, or amplify sound.
- The closest commercial comparator, Neosensory, has shut down. The report does not treat that as proof the technology failed. It treats the standalone direct-to-consumer model as the part that did not survive.
- An EssilorLuxottica acquisition of that technology is reported by one investor source and is not confirmed in the review. Do not state it as fact.
- Regulatory class follows the stated purpose. Positioning as environmental awareness and alerting is a different door from positioning as a device that compensates for hearing impairment.
- The remaining job is evidence that this implementation helps in real use, not another argument that the problem exists.
